Spellbound Sweets Sounds

What the haunted bowl sounds like. Every sound here is what the game plays. Start with the demo round, then pick the ones you like below.

The demo round

90 seconds of a pretend game: the skull asleep, a hand creeping in, dodges and a feint, a hand in the bowl, the horns, the slide, crying, the music box, and the restart. Tap the bar or a line to jump there.

    Every sound

    Each number is one take. The game picks a different take each time, never the same one twice in a row, with a little change of pitch and level. The dot is the face's mood colour when it plays.
