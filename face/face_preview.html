Creature Face

Your two 16-LED rings and the 16 by 8 mouth, shown LED for LED. Move your pointer over the bench as the hand. Reach into the bowl and it snarls.

Mood

LEDs lit: 0 of 160 Draw: 0.00 A at 5 V

Chosen: sleeping for waiting, clenched teeth with level brows for watching, the snapping jaw for snarl, crying for gives up. Each mood loops for as long as it lasts. Ring LED 0 is drawn at the top, counting clockwise; the real rings may start somewhere else. The draw figure assumes about 20 mA per colour at full brightness.
